# TASK-0134 — Supplier linking: manual out-of-stock per variant, device limit (count, unlimited, not specified) and unspecified licence period, and suggestions ranked by parsed years/devices in supplier line names

## Objective

Owner request, 2026-10-26, from the supplier linking page. Three parts:

1. A button to mark a variant out of stock when the supplier no longer offers it, or it has left the list.
2. Product years and devices chosen from lists, including "unlimited" and "not specified / not applicable", instead of typed a different way each time.
3. Link suggestions that understand the years and devices in supplier line names. The owner's example: our "3 years · 2 devices" was offered "Key 3 Device 2 Year" first.

Owner decisions (in chat, 2026-10-26):

- The manual flag stays until "back in stock". The sync never clears it.
- Devices are chosen as a count, unlimited, or not specified.
- A suggestion whose terms differ stays visible, shown below the matching ones and flagged, not hidden.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Manual out-of-stock.** `PUT /v1/admin/supplier/stock/:variantId {outOfStock}` (ADMIN, CATALOG) sets `Variant.manualOutOfStock` and audits it.
   - The flag feeds `supplierOutOfStock` through `reconcileStock`. The product page, cart, checkout and facets therefore treat the variant as unbuyable without any change of their own.
   - A sync never clears it. "Back in stock" emails the people who asked while the variant was marked out (`manualOutOfStockAt`).
2. **Terms data.**
   - `LicensePeriodUnit.UNSPECIFIED` is treated like LIFETIME for expiry: no renewal reminders, no "for you" renewal entries.
   - `Variant.deviceCount` is nullable. Null means not specified; 0 keeps meaning unlimited.
   - Contracts, the edit and create services, the AI draft, the product card and the SEO scripts all handle both.
3. **Admin pickers.** The new-product form, add variant, edit variant and create-from-line use dropdowns:
   - presets for the term and the device count;
   - lifetime or unlimited;
   - not specified;
   - "another…" for a custom value.
4. **Suggestions.**
   - Line names are parsed for devices (PC, device, user, seat, mac, unlimited, 1D1Y) and for the term (years, months, days, lifetime). A part the name doesn't state is UNKNOWN.
   - Lines whose terms match come first; lines that differ are flagged ≠.
   - A note appears when every suggestion differs, and the linked line is flagged when its terms differ.
   - The AI draft prefers the terms parsed from the line's name.
5. **Storefront.** A term or device count that is not specified is hidden from the variant label and the specs ("Standard edition" when both are missing). Unlimited reads as "Unlimited".
6. **Checks.** Rendered at 1440/1024/768/390/360 in ar (RTL) and en with no horizontal overflow.

## Migration plan

`20261026100000_variant_terms_manual_stock` is additive only:

- an enum value;
- a dropped NOT NULL on `deviceCount`;
- `manualOutOfStock` (default false) and `manualOutOfStockAt`.

Existing rows keep their values. The drift check is clean against a disposable Postgres 18.

**Apply it to staging before merging.** Coolify does not run migrations.

**Rollback (forward-only):**

1. A migration converts UNSPECIFIED terms to LIFETIME (value null), which the older code accepts (REV-0190).
2. It sets null device counts back to 1 and restores NOT NULL.
3. It drops the two columns.
4. The enum value stays, unused.
