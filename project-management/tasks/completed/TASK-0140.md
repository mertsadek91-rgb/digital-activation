# TASK-0140 — Product images fill their frame: square, unpadded image boxes on the product page gallery and on product cards (rails, listings)

## Objective

Owner, 2026-10-27, with two screenshots (product page gallery; home "most bought"
rail): the square product render floats inside a larger, lighter rounded frame with
empty bands around it. Either the picture's background fills the frame, or the frame
fits the picture.

## Approach

Every catalogue picture on staging is a square render (1200x1200 or 1254x1254, measured
on new.digital-activation.com), so the frame is made the picture's own square:

- `.card-media`: `aspect-ratio: 1` instead of a fixed 200px/150px height, no padding on
  the box or the image, `object-fit: cover`. `sizes` 260px -> 280px (widest card is 281px
  at 1024).
- `.gallery`: square, full column width on desktop (560px at 1440, 472px at 1024); one
  column below 900px, so it is capped at 400px (tablet) and kept at 220px on phones, as
  TASK-0122 set it, and centred with the thumbnails. `sizes` follows those widths.
- Thumbnails: same treatment (no 4px inset, `cover`, clipped to the radius).
- The gradient stays as the background of the no-picture drawing only.
- Overlays: on phones the "instant" and discount pills are tighter so they fit side by
  side on a 130px picture. Pre-existing bug fixed on the way: `direction: ltr` on the
  discount pill also flipped its logical inset, so in Arabic it sat under the instant
  pill and was invisible; the number is now isolated by an inner `dir="ltr"` span.
