# TASK-0136 — Product agent: DB-backed batch queue worked one product at a time with a cooldown, AI category decision, bilingual copy saved through the existing editors, logo discovery with SSRF guard and colour-matched card, auto-publish when the gate passes, per-item undo, admin screen to pick products/supplier lines and follow batches

## Objective

Owner request in chat, 2026-10-26 (CR-0007): an AI agent inside the panel that works on many products at once. The owner picks products from the database with tick boxes and presses start; the agent then works through them on its own:

- **Rewrite** products already in the catalogue, or **create** new ones from supplier lines no product is linked to yet.
- Write the page in **Arabic and English**.
- Keep, correct or **create the section** a product belongs in.
- **Find the brand's logo** on its official site, take its colours, and design the product card in them.
- **Approve automatically**: the owner follows up afterwards rather than reviewing each one.
- **Wait between products**, so the big model calls are not all sent at once.

The owner asked for no questions and the best decisions; those taken are below.

## Decisions taken (owner delegated them)

1. **One product at a time, with a cooldown.** The default is 90 s between products (30–900 s, per batch), which is about 40 products an hour. A rate limit, an overloaded provider or a timeout puts the product back in the queue and holds the whole batch for 5 minutes. A product gets at most 3 tries.
2. **A DB-backed queue in the API**, not in-memory jobs:
   - `AgentBatch` and `AgentItem`, worked by an interval worker under an advisory lock;
   - one RUNNING item across all replicas;
   - a heartbeat recovers items lost to a restart.
3. **Writes go through the editors' own services**:
   - CatalogEditService (create, sections);
   - ProductContentService and AdminService.setProductCopy (copy);
   - TaxonomyService (new section);
   - CardService (card);
   - SupplierService.linkBySku;
   - AdminService.setStatus (publish gate).

   So validation, sanitising, the publish gate and the audit log apply exactly as they do to a person's edit. The audit actor is the person who started the batch.

4. **"Approve automatically" means publish only when the publish gate passes.** Otherwise the product stays a draft, with the gate's reasons in the item. The gate is never bypassed.
5. **Undo per product.** A rewrite saves a snapshot before its first write:
   - copy, sections and status;
   - translations and pictures it adds.

   Undo restores all of it. A created product goes back to draft and is never deleted. Undo is offered only on the latest item that changed a product.

6. **One planning call per product.** It decides the section, the names, the brand's website and the card's words together, so the only other model calls are the two page copies (and the draft for a new product). Section ids the model invents are dropped. A section with the same name is reused, never duplicated.
7. **Logo search is SSRF-guarded.** The domain comes from a model, so the fetch:
   - allows https only, on port 443, and no IP literals;
   - checks every address in the socket's own DNS lookup, refusing private, loopback, link-local, CGNAT, mapped and NAT64 ranges, so there is no rebinding gap;
   - re-checks each of at most 3 redirects;
   - caps the size and has a deadline.

   SVG is rasterised only when it cannot reach outside the file. Candidates, best first:
   1. the header logo;
   2. the manifest icons;
   3. the touch icon;
   4. the favicon;
   5. Google's favicon service.

8. **CREATE skips** a supplier line that is already linked, and a line whose drafted name matches an existing product (linking it is a person's call, CR-0004).
9. **Roles.** Starting and retrying a batch is ADMIN-only, since it spends the OpenCode balance and publishes. ADMIN and CATALOG can read, pause, cancel and undo. `PRODUCT_AGENT=off` (or `CRON_JOBS=off`) stops the worker.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `/agent` in the panel has three tabs:
   - **Rewrite products**: catalogue products with search and filters (published, drafts, no image, thin content, never run).
   - **Add new products**: unlinked supplier lines with search and filters (in stock, no wholesale, never run).
   - **Batches**.
2. Each picker has tick boxes, "select shown" and a 200-product cap.
3. Each picker has options: copy, section fix, new sections, card mode, auto-publish, batch notes and cooldown. Start is confirmed.
4. The batches tab shows:
   - progress and the countdown to the next product;
   - pause, resume and cancel;
   - per product, its step, outcome (section, languages, card with logo source and colours, published or the gate's reasons), log, retry and undo.
5. The worker:
   - claims one item at a time under an advisory lock and refuses while one is RUNNING;
   - honours `nextRunAt`;
   - recovers stale RUNNING items;
   - marks batches DONE.
6. A rewrite:
   - adds a missing translation;
   - applies the plan's section (keep, assign or create);
   - writes both languages;
   - draws the card (missing or always);
   - publishes only through the gate;
   - saves a snapshot, and undo restores it exactly.
7. A create drafts, creates, links the line, and then does the same as a rewrite. A retry or rate-limit requeue carries on with the product already made.
8. A transient model error requeues the item with a 5-minute batch backoff. Other errors fail the item, and retry queues it again.
9. Checks:
   - unit tests for the SSRF guard, logo ranking, SVG safety, palette, plan checking, transient detection and the contract;
   - an integration test against Postgres 18 for rewrite + undo, create + link + skip, rate limit + cancel, and the start refusal;
   - typecheck and lint clean;
   - the panel rendered in ar (RTL) and en at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 360.

## Migration plan

`20261026140000_product_agent` is additive only: three enums and two new tables, which nothing else references. It applies cleanly with `migrate deploy` on a disposable Postgres 18, and the integration suite runs against it.

**Apply it to staging before merging** (owner approval). Coolify does not run migrations. If the code lands first, the worker logs the missing tables once an hour and does nothing; the /agent screen shows an error until the migration is applied.

**Rollback (forward-only):** a migration that drops both tables and the enums.
