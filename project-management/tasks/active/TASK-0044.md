# TASK-0044 — Staging becomes production in place

## Task objective

The one new-stack environment is both staging and the future production.
Write the list of everything that changes when it stops being staging.

## Scope

- **Demo data out:** the `example.invalid` customers and orders, test keys, demo
  reviews, the demo promotion. Also a decision on the one non-demo customer and
  order.
- **Environment:**
  - storefront domain moves from `new.` to the apex
  - `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (switches indexing on)
  - `STOREFRONT_URL` (CORS and email links)
  - `SEO_BLOCK_INDEXING`
  - `PREVIEW_TOKEN` removed
- **Mail and payments:** confirm the production mail transport and senders;
  switch payment keys to live (after CONSENSUS-0001).
- **Sweeps:** decide whether cart recovery and invitations should run against
  staging data until then.

## Acceptance criteria

- The list is reviewed by pm-04 and pm-06 and folded into the cutover runbook
  (TASK-0032).
- The demo-data removal is written as a dry-run-by-default script. Nobody runs
  it without owner approval.
