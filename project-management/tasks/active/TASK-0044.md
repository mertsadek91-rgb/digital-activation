# TASK-0044 — Staging becomes production in place

## Task objective

The one new-stack environment is both staging and the future production.
Write the list of everything that changes when it stops being staging.

## Scope

- **Demo data out:** the `example.invalid` customers and orders, test keys, demo
  reviews, the demo promotion. Also a decision on the one non-demo customer and
  order.
- **Environment:**
  - storefront domain moves from `new.` to the apex
  - `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (switches indexing on)
  - `STOREFRONT_URL` (CORS and email links)
  - `SEO_BLOCK_INDEXING`
  - `PREVIEW_TOKEN` removed
- **Mail and payments:** confirm the production mail transport and senders;
  switch payment keys to live (after CONSENSUS-0001).
- **Sweeps:** decide whether cart recovery and invitations should run against
  staging data until then.

### 4. Recommendation (release-manager support — a recommendation, not a decision)

- **Do not fix a date yet.** Six blockers are open (P1–P6) plus the invalid
  review round (P10). The shortest path is P1, P2 and P5, because they decide
  what the database looks like on day one.
- **Launch with whatever payment method is proven end to end with a real
  order**; bank transfer alone is acceptable if Stripe is not approved, rather
  than delaying.
- **Wipe demo and test data only**, with a backup and the dry-run script; keep
  append-only history (`AuditLog`, `StockMovement`) as history.
- **Do not rotate secrets at cutover** unless a leak is suspected; set
  `LINK_SIGNING_SECRET` beforehand instead.
- **Index from day one** (no hold): the redirects are proven and a hold delays
  recovery of the search traffic the redirects are meant to carry.
- **Window:** a weekday early morning store time, Monday to Wednesday, with the
  owner and an engineer on hand for 72 hours.
