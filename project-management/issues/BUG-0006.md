## Actual

`GET /v1/orders/:number` accepts the cart cookie, a customer session or the HMAC `?key=`; `POST /v1/orders/:number/pay` accepts only the cart cookie (checkout.controller.ts ~140). A buyer who opens the payment email on their phone sees the order and cannot pay.

## Why it is not simply a bug fix

Letting the link pay widens what a forwarded email can do. That is a product and security trade-off (pm-01 + pm-06), not an engineering default.
