## Actual

- `common/order-link.ts`: the `?key=` HMAC has no expiry. The page shows no licence keys, which bounds the impact.
- The HMAC for order and cart-restore links uses `JWT_ACCESS_SECRET` with distinct labels, so rotating the session secret invalidates every emailed link, and the two purposes share one key.
- `JWT_REFRESH_SECRET` is validated at boot and never used (refresh tokens are random, not JWTs).
